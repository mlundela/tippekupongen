# altomfotball

Football data comes from the NIFS API (`v2api.nifs.no`). Before calling it or writing code against it, read [`docs/nifs-api.md`](docs/nifs-api.md): request rules (cache-poisoning 406s, rate limit, silent route fallback), routes, ids, match-event codes, recipes for tables, fixtures, match reports and player stats.

Tipping coupons come from the Norsk Tipping API. Before using it or linking its teams or matches to NIFS, read [`docs/norsk-tipping-api.md`](docs/norsk-tipping-api.md): the endpoint shape, the NT→NIFS match join, and the team map in `data/nt-nifs-teams.json`.

## Coupon pages

One phone-first Norwegian page per Norsk Tipping coupon (`SATURDAY`, `SUNDAY`, `MIDWEEK`). Python 3 stdlib only.

1. `python3 -m altomfotball facts DAY` writes `coupons/<salestop-date>-<day>/facts.json`: per match, both teams' table, home/away records, all-competition form, goal timing, player stats, injuries and suspensions, head-to-head, and Oddsen H/U/B odds ([`docs/oddsen-api.md`](docs/oddsen-api.md)).
2. Write `coupons/<…>/analysis.json`: our *reading* of the numbers, not a restatement of them. The page shows no raw stats and no probabilities of our own; Oddsen's odds are the reference. Bokmål for a Norwegian tipping audience. Before writing or rewriting the text, read [`docs/sprak.md`](docs/sprak.md): voice, headlines, the vurdering, and stats in plain words.
   - `_coupon`: `headline`, `intro` (paragraphs): where the public and the odds disagree most.
   - Per match, keyed by NT `gameEngineEventId`: `headline`, `take` (two paragraphs), `signal` (`[{stat, text}]`, shown as «Tallene som støtter folket»: data that *supports the public's split*, i.e. why the public's favourite is favourite, or, where the public backs a different sign or is surer than the odds, the case for that), `noise` (`[{stat, text}]`, shown as «Dette kan snu kampen»: what could *turn the match against the public*, such as luck that may run out, absences, fatigue, or what the odds see that the public doesn't), `crowd` (how the public's split differs from the odds). Weave each team's last match (any competition, `facts.json` `lastMatch` from FotMob: score, scorers, xG, shots, big chances, red cards) into `take` where it says something about the team; there is no separate recap section.
   - Separate quality from luck with `underlying` in `facts.json`: xG vs goals, shots on target for and against, conversion, save %, PDO (1,00 is neutral), plus `restDays` and `table.withdrawnPoints`. Compare with league averages (≈0,34 goals per shot on target).
   - Naming a *key player* in the text gives them a card with league stats from `facts.json` `players` (`render._mentioned()`). A key player has started at least half the team's league matches, or has goals + assists ≥ 3 and ≥ 15 % of the team's league goals (`render.is_key_player()`). Other players can be named but get no card. Each card shows 1–3 stars from the player's rank on FotMob's season rating for the league (`facts.json` `players[].seasonRating`, `render.stars()`: top 15 % ★★★, top 50 % ★★, else ★); players under FotMob's minutes threshold get no stars. Use the full name on first mention; a bare surname only matches when no other player on the two teams shares it.
   - The tendens is computed by `render.system()` from `facts.json` `odds.implied` against the public's split: `tendens()` per match, then at least `MIN_BOMBS` bombs (signs the public gives under `BOMB_BELOW` %) at the same row count. Don't write one; if the text names the system (spikes, bombs), check it against the rendered coupon.
   - Every number must come from `facts.json`. Check each claim against it before rendering, and re-run `facts` first if tables may have moved (Friday matches).
3. `python3 -m altomfotball render DAY` writes `site/<…>.html`.

Injuries and suspensions come from FotMob ([`docs/fotmob-api.md`](docs/fotmob-api.md)), stored per team as `unavailable` and as `status` on each player in `facts.json`. Card risk is still computed from NIFS with `BAN_THRESHOLDS` in `altomfotball/analysis.py` (Eliteserien: every 4th yellow, NFF Reaksjonsreglement § 4-7). Without FotMob data the absences fall back to inference (`absenceSource: "beregnet"`), and the text must say so.
